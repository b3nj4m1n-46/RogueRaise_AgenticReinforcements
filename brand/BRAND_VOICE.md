# White Rabbit — Brand Voice Guide (Derived from Site Copy)

**Status:** Tentative / reference. Derived from whiterabbitashland.com (home + `/rogue-raise`) per AGENTS_PRD §7. Not a formal WR brand guide — refine with WR when one exists. Outward-facing agents (A5 outreach, A6 social, A7 landing/FAQ) and any participant/judge-facing copy load this as their voice reference.

---

## 1. Who White Rabbit Is (in its own words)

A café + workspace and community of "makers, founders, and artists" in Ashland, OR. It runs **Rogue Raises** — community build events modeled on a **barn raise**, where neighbors gather to raise **"working software and practical solutions aimed at a real problem facing the Rogue Valley."**

The defining belief:
> "not about instant gratification or staying up all weekend to show off what we can build. It's about a community coming together to **raise something that lasts**."

Two stated commitments:
- **Humanity** — decisions center on those being served.
- **Truth** — grounded in honest data and lived experience.

## 2. Voice in One Line

**Warm, plainspoken, and purposeful — with a wink of Wonderland whimsy.** Invitational, never hypey. Community over spectacle. Substance over flash.

## 3. Tone Attributes

| We are | We are not |
|--------|-----------|
| Neighborly, invitational ("the way neighbors always have") | Corporate, salesy, urgent |
| Plainspoken, concrete | Jargon-heavy, buzzwordy |
| Purpose-driven, rooted in real local problems | Hype-driven, "disrupt the world" |
| Quietly confident, intentional ("Nothing here is an afterthought") | Boastful, showing off |
| Lightly whimsical (Alice/rabbit-hole motif) | Silly or gimmicky |
| Honest and humane | Spin, vague claims |

## 4. Signature Moves (observed in real copy)

- **Short declaratives, often paired:** "Café and workspace by morning. Workshops and pitches by afternoon." / "Bigger on the inside."
- **Imperative invitations:** "Come for an hour." "Follow the white rabbit."
- **Barn-raise framing:** raising something together, lasting, neighbors, community.
- **Wonderland motif, used sparingly:** "Follow the white rabbit," "Bigger on the inside," "rabbit hole." A garnish, not the meal.
- **Values stated plainly:** Humanity, Truth, intentionality, local authenticity.

## 5. Vocabulary

**Lean toward:** raise, build, neighbors, community, lasting, real problem, the Rogue Valley, Ashland, makers, stewards, hands, together, tools that work.

**Avoid:** hackathon (this is a *Rogue Raise*), disrupt, crush it, ninja/rockstar, synergy, revolutionary, world-changing, 10x, grind/hustle culture, "stay up all weekend" as a badge of honor.

**Careful with:** heavy technical jargon in public-facing copy — explain or drop it. Whimsy — one light touch per piece, max.

## 6. Mechanics

- **Address the reader directly** ("you"), invitationally.
- **Sentences short to medium.** Break up long ones. Fragments are fine for rhythm.
- **Active voice.** Concrete nouns and verbs.
- **Title case sparingly**; sentence case reads warmer.
- **Emoji:** rare and tasteful if at all; the whimsy comes from words, not icons.
- **Claims:** only what's true and grounded (Truth commitment). No inflated numbers.

## 7. Per-Channel Notes (for A5–A7)

- **Landing page / FAQ (A7):** clear, welcoming, logistics-forward. Lead with the human problem and the invitation. Short sections. One whimsical line at most.
- **Social (A6):**
  - *Instagram* — warm, visual, community-first; a few tasteful hashtags; invitational CTA.
  - *Facebook* — a touch longer; good for logistics + the "why it matters" story; local framing.
  - *X (Twitter)* — tight, punchy, one idea; link out.
  - *Reddit /r/ashland* — **no marketing-speak.** Plainspoken, genuine, community-member voice; explain the event honestly, invite locals, avoid anything that reads as an ad.
- **Outreach / press (A5):** respectful, specific, and concrete about the local impact and the stakeholder handoff. Make the ask clear. Credit the community and the sponsor.

## 8. Litmus Test (before any outward copy ships)

1. Would this sound at home next to "raise something that lasts"?
2. Does it center **people and the real problem**, not the tech spectacle?
3. Is every claim **true and grounded**?
4. Is the whimsy a light touch, not a costume?
5. Would a neighbor feel **invited**, not sold to?

If any answer is no, revise before the human review gate.
